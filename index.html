☼ PHOSPHORSOL

Real-Time Space Weather Monitor.

Monitor live space weather conditions through the warm glow of a vintage phosphor terminal. PhosphorSol pulls real-time data from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center and renders solar wind, geomagnetic indices, X-ray flux, aurora forecasts, and storm-scale alerts in authentic retro CRT style.

FEATURES

  • Live NOAA/SWPC space weather scales: R (radio blackout), S (solar radiation), G (geomagnetic storm)
  • Real-time solar wind speed and IMF Bz from DSCOVR satellite
  • Kp index trend charts with observed and forecast values
  • GOES X-ray flux with flare classification and recent flare event log
  • Aurora probability forecast with OVATION auroral oval imagery
  • SWPC alert and warning log with 30-day history
  • Green phosphor and amber CRT colour themes
  • No accounts, no tracking, no clutter

DATA SOURCE

All space weather data is provided by NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) via their public APIs and data services.
